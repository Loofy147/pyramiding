Strategy inputs

Account & margin
Fee and slippage are charged on entry, adds and modeled exit. Maintenance stays a flat-rate approximation; exchange tiers are not loaded.
Pyramiding rules
Sizing is clipped by the safety-gap gate and maximum initial-margin usage. A rejected add does not increase exposure.
Exit gates
Set hard stop to 0 to disable it. Stop exits close the modeled portfolio and pay an estimated exit fee.
Market & Monte Carlo
Both assets share one account wallet. Return and volatility inputs apply per step. Hours per step controls funding accrual and converts daily shock probability into step probability.
Funding & tail-risk stress
Funding is charged on average open notional per step; negative rates credit the account. Common shocks hit both assets together. Shock probability defaults to 0% so base runs are not silently stress-biased.

Model scope & known limitations

Modeled: two long-only linear assets with shared equity, a flat maintenance rate, per-side fee and slippage costs, signed funding accrual from average pre/post-step marks, correlated synthetic log returns, optional common tail shocks, add safety gates, exit rules, liquidation/ruin tracking, percentile bands and spot baseline.

Not modeled: exchange-specific risk tiers, partial liquidation, liquidation penalties, order-book fill impact, outages, shorts/hedges or live market data. Slippage is an additive cost, not a changed fill price. Funding is a constant configured rate approximated from average notional; tail shocks are stress assumptions. Liquidation is conservatively treated as total modeled equity loss.

For exchange-specific context, see the official rule descriptions. Their examples and formulas are venue- and contract-dependent:
